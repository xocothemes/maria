## What this site stores

This site stores two small preference records in your browser: your color theme and your cookie choices. They are saved in `localStorage`, are not sent with page requests, and only remember your choices in this browser.

| Key                    | Type          | Category  | Purpose                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `maria-theme`          | Local storage | Essential | Remembers whether you picked light or dark mode                       |
| `maria-cookie-consent` | Local storage | Essential | Remembers your consent choices, when you made them, and their version |

## Consent categories

Essential storage is always on, because it remembers the two choices above. Analytics and marketing are optional and stay off until you allow them.

No analytics or marketing tools are connected by default. The categories exist so tools added later only load with your consent.

## Changing your preferences

You can change your choices at any time with the **Cookie preferences** button in the footer, or <button type="button" class="link" data-cookie-preferences>open your cookie preferences</button> now.

## For site owners

The consent script exposes `window.mariaCookieConsent` so optional scripts can check whether analytics or marketing is allowed before they load, and dispatches a `maria:cookieConsentChanged` event whenever a visitor saves new choices.
